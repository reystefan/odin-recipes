Four Cheese Pizza

Description

A classic four cheese pizza, also known as pizza quattro formaggi, is a rich and comforting dish beloved by cheese enthusiasts. This recipe combines mild mozzarella, savory provolone, sharp parmesan, and tangy gorgonzola over a light layer of tomato sauce.

Baked until the crust turns golden and the cheese blend melts into bubbly perfection, it delivers an irresistible balance of textures and bold flavors in every single slice.

Ingredients

  • 1 ball of pizza dough (store-bought or homemade)
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup crumbled gorgonzola cheese
  • 1/2 cup shredded provolone cheese
  • 1/4 cup freshly grated parmesan cheese
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano (optional)
  • Fresh basil leaves for garnish (optional)

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven: Heat your oven to 240°C (475°F). If using a pizza stone, place it inside while preheating.
  2. Shape the dough: Roll or stretch out the pizza dough on a lightly floured surface into a 30 cm (12-inch) round circle.
  3. Add the base: Transfer the dough to a baking sheet (or pizza peel) and spread the tomato sauce evenly across the surface, leaving a small border for the crust.
  4. Layer the cheeses: Distribute the shredded mozzarella and provolone evenly over the sauce. Scatter pieces of crumbled gorgonzola throughout, and finish by sprinkling the grated parmesan cheese on top.
  5. Drizzle oil: Lightly drizzle the tablespoon of olive oil over the cheese and dust with dried oregano if desired.
  6. Bake: Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, or until the crust is crisp and golden brown and the cheese is melted and bubbling.
  7. Garnish and serve: Remove from the oven, top with fresh basil leaves if using, slice, and serve hot.
